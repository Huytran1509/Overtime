Japan's world champion Yamaguchi Akane claimed the women's singles crown at the BWF World Tour Super 1000 China Open in Changzhou, defeating home favourite Chen Yufei in just 44 minutes. In the men's final, Chinese Taipei veteran Chou Tien-chen beat France's Toma Junior Popov in three games to claim his first Super 1000 title since the 2019 Indonesia Open. Popov's run to the final was historic, making him the first French men's player to reach a Super 1000 final.
