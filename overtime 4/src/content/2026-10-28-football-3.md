Bayern Munich striker Harry Kane was named the winner of the 70th Ballon d'Or at a ceremony held at the London Palladium on October 26, becoming the first English player to claim the award since Michael Owen in 2001. Kane registered 73 goals in 65 appearances for club and country during the 2025-26 season and beat out Kylian Mbappe and Lamine Yamal for the prize. Spain's Alexia Putellas claimed the women's award for a record third time. The ceremony was held in London for the first time in the award's history, in tribute to inaugural winner Stanley Matthews.
