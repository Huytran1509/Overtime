India's Lakshya Sen stormed into the semifinals of the BWF World Tour Super 500 Hong Kong Open after defeating former world champion Loh Kean Yew of Singapore 21-17, 10-21, 21-13 in the quarterfinals. The victory extended his unbeaten run to 13 consecutive matches on tour, marking his third straight semifinal appearance. Sen will face Denmark's third seed Anders Antonsen in the last four on Saturday.
