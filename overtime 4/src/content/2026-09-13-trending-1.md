The first-ever World Athletics Ultimate Championship wrapped up on September 13 in Budapest, Hungary, with three days of elite track and field action at the National Athletics Centre. The invitational event featured Olympic and world champions across 28 disciplines competing for a record $10 million prize purse, with individual event winners taking home $150,000. American Melissa Jefferson-Wooden won the inaugural women's 100m title in 10.62 seconds — the joint seventh-fastest of all time — while Armand Duplantis took the men's pole vault with a clearance of 6.20m. The event also saw standout performances from Marileidy Paulino in the women's 400m and a strong showing from Masai Russell in the 100m hurdles.
