Lionel Messi announced his retirement from international football on August 31, ending 21 years of service with the Argentina national team. The 39-year-old made the announcement via Instagram, citing that the time had come to step away after leading Argentina to the 2022 World Cup title and the 2026 World Cup final, where they lost 1-0 to Spain in extra time. Messi, who scored 98 goals in 142 international appearances, will continue his club career with Inter Miami in MLS.
