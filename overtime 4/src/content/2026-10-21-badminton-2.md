India's armless para-archer Sheetal Devi reached the women's compound final at the 2026 Asian Para Games in Nagoya, defeating Japan's Shinohara Aya 145-137 in the semi-final. During the elimination rounds she set a new world record, scoring 149 points against Hong Kong's Chan Yuen Wah. She will contest the gold-medal match against Singapore's Alim Nur Syahidah on October 22, with India's overall tally standing at 35 medals after two days of competition.
