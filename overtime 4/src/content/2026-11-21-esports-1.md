The Esports Foundation announced an expansion of the qualifying structure for the 2027 Esports Nations Cup following a qualification season that attracted nearly 200,000 players across 210 regional events. Around 2,000 players from 123 countries and territories secured tournament slots, and all national team slots earned in 2026 will be retained for 2027. Coaches will also be permitted to expand their national rosters, with updated lists due by January 2027.
