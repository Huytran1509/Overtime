During his tenure he led United to four cup finals, winning the 2024 Women's FA Cup — the club's first major honour — and took them to the Champions League quarter-finals last season. His exit came after ongoing tensions over squad investment, with United finishing fourth in the WSL last season and missing out on European qualification.
