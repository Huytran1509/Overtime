The 2026 Li-Ning Cup National Badminton Team Championship concluded in Shenyang, the capital of northeast China's Liaoning Province, marking the third consecutive year the city hosted the event. Twenty-one elite provincial teams and nearly 300 professional athletes competed at the Liaoning Gymnasium for team honours. Shenyang used the event to promote an integrated approach to culture, tourism, sports, and commerce under its 'Travelling with Sports Events' initiative.
