The 30th edition of the BWF World Championships got underway on August 17 at the Indira Gandhi Arena in New Delhi, India — the country's first time hosting the event since 2009. First and second round matches are being played on August 18, with 64 men's and women's singles players, plus 48 pairs across three doubles disciplines, all competing for the top prize. Top seeds An Se-young of South Korea and Shi Yuqi of China lead the women's and men's singles draws respectively, with the finals scheduled for August 23.
