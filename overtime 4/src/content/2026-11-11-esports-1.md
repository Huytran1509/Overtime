Bilibili Gaming defeated T1 three games to one in the second semifinal of the 2026 League of Legends World Championship, ending T1's remarkable run of 13 consecutive series wins against LPL teams at Worlds stretching back to 2013. BLG had previously beaten T1 at the 2026 Mid-Season Invitational but had lost the 2024 Worlds final to them in five games. The Chinese squad, led by mid laner Knight and top laner Bin, will now face Gen.G in the grand final at Barclays Center in New York on November 14.
