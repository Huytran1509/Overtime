The 2026 AFL Women's season — the eleventh edition of the competition — is set to begin on August 9 with a headline double-header at Marvel Stadium in Melbourne, featuring St Kilda hosting Carlton. The 18-club season will run through late November, with a representative Australian vs Ireland international match also played on August 1 at North Sydney Oval, the first AFLW representative fixture since 2017. The season opener will be broadcast live on Fox Footy, Kayo Sports, and Binge.
