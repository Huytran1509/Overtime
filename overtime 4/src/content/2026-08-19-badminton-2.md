The 2026 BWF World Championships women's singles draw features five former world champions among the top 16 seeds, raising the stakes for the tournament in New Delhi. Three-time defending champion Akane Yamaguchi of Japan is seeded second behind top seed An Seyoung, while Ratchanok Intanon, PV Sindhu, and Nozomi Okuhara also feature. The quarterfinals are scheduled for August 21 and the finals for August 23.
