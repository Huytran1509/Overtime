A total of 11 medal events across 23 nations are on the program, featuring titles including League of Legends, PUBG Mobile, Honor of Kings, and Pokémon Unite. The tournament, co-organized by the Japan Esports Union and the Asian Electronic Sports Federation, serves as a key test of esports' long-term place in the Asian Games program.
