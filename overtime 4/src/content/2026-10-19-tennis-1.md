Poland's Maja Chwalinska claimed her maiden WTA Tour title at the 2026 Wuhan Open, defeating Britain's Sonay Kartal 2-6, 6-4, 6-2 in the final on October 18. It was a remarkable achievement for the 25-year-old, who became only the fourth player ever to win a WTA 1000 event as her first tour-level singles title. The result propels Chwalinska into the world's top 11, capping a dream season that already included a run to the French Open final.
