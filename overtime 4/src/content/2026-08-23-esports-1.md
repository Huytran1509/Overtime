The 2026 Esports World Cup, held in Paris from 6 July to 23 August, came to a close on Sunday with the final Trophy Award Ceremony at the Grand Palais. The event featured 25 tournaments across 24 esport titles, with a record $75 million prize pool distributed among top organisations including Team Falcons, Team Vitality, Team Spirit, The MongolZ, Natus Vincere, and FURIA.
