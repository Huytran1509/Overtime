Bilibili Gaming defeated Gen.G 3-1 in the League of Legends World Championship 2026 Grand Final at Barclays Center in Brooklyn, New York on 14 November. It is BLG's first world title and returns the Summoner's Cup to the LPL for the first time since EDward Gaming in 2021, ending a four-year run of LCK dominance. Mid laner Knight was named Finals MVP, becoming the first Chinese player ever to win that award at a Worlds final. BLG eliminated all four Korean teams on their path to the title, including T1 in the semifinals.
