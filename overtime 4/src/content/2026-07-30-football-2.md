FIFA announced plans to create a semi-private subsidiary called FIFA Forward Enterprise (FFE) that would manage the commercial rights to the World Cup and other competitions, with up to a 20 percent stake offered to external investors. UEFA immediately condemned the move, saying the World Cup is not FIFA's to sell, and is reportedly planning an emergency meeting to discuss opposition including a potential boycott. Former FIFA president Sepp Blatter also blasted the proposal, saying it would strip football of its soul and that the tournament belongs to the people, not a handful of executives. Critics also raised concerns about potential investor ties to US President Donald Trump.
