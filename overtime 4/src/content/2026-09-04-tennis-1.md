Novak Djokovic suffered a shocking first-round defeat at the 2026 US Open, falling to unseeded Argentine Mariano Navone in a five-set marathon lasting four hours and 36 minutes. It was the 39-year-old Serb's earliest exit at a Grand Slam since the 2006 Australian Open, ending his 77-match Grand Slam first-round winning streak. The loss further delays Djokovic's pursuit of a record 25th Grand Slam title until at least 2027. Hours after the defeat, Djokovic confirmed he will enter the China Open, returning to Beijing for the first time since 2015.
