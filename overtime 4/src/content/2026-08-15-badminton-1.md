The 30th edition of the BWF World Championships is set to get underway in New Delhi, India, from August 17 to 23, 2026. It is India's first time hosting the prestigious event in 17 years, returning to Asia for the first time since 2018. All five disciplines — men's and women's singles, men's and women's doubles, and mixed doubles — will be contested at the Indira Gandhi Arena.
