Malaysian shuttler Lee Zii Jia defeated European champion Christo Popov of France in straight games in just 38 minutes to reach the semi-finals of the BWF Arctic Open 2026 in Vantaa, Finland. It is only the second semi-final Lee has reached all year following two injury-hit seasons since his Paris 2024 Olympic bronze. He will next face Denmark's Anders Antonsen, with whom he holds a level career record of 5-5.
