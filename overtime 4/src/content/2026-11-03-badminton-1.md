India enjoyed a historic double at the HYLO Open 2026 BWF Super 500 event in Saarbrucken, Germany, with both Lakshya Sen and PV Sindhu winning their respective singles finals. Sen defeated Indonesia's Jonatan Christie 21-17, 21-10 in just 39 minutes to secure his first BWF title of the season. Sindhu beat Denmark's Line Kjaersfeldt 21-12, 21-14 to claim her third final appearance of the year, marking the first time since 2022 that two Indians have won titles at the same Super 500 or higher event.
