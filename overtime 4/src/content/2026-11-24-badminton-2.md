Indonesia has secured three representatives for the 2026 BWF World Tour Finals, with Alwi Farhan qualifying in men's singles for the first time in his career. The women's doubles pair of Tiwi and Fadia also confirmed their spots after reaching the semi-finals of the 2026 Hong Kong Open. BWF is set to send out official invitations on November 24, with a response deadline of November 27 and the group-stage draw scheduled for December 5.
