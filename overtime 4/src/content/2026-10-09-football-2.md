The Court of Arbitration for Sport held a one-day hearing in Lausanne on October 8 over Senegal's appeal against being stripped of the 2025 Africa Cup of Nations title. Senegal won the final 1-0 against hosts Morocco after extra time, but the Confederation of African Football's Appeal Board later ruled Senegal had forfeited the match due to a 17-minute walkoff in protest of a disallowed goal and a VAR call. CAS director general Mathieu Reeb said a final decision will come as soon as possible.
