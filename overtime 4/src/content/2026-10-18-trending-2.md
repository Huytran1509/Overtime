India's contingent of 168 athletes, 112 male and 56 female, has officially begun competing at the 2026 Asian Para Games in Japan, running from October 18 to 24. Sprinter Simran Sharma was announced as co-flag bearer alongside Pramod Bhagat at the opening ceremony, replacing Bhavina Patel. India's squad is smaller than the 303 athletes sent to the previous edition in Hangzhou in 2023.
