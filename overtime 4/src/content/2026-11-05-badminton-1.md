Fifteen-year-old Tanvi Patri became the first Indian badminton player to reach a Youth Olympic Games final in women's singles, guaranteeing India's first-ever medal in the category at Dakar 2026. She defeated Japan's second seed Yuzuno Watanabe 21-14, 11-21, 21-19 in a dramatic 54-minute semi-final. Patri will face China's Yi Xin Zhang in the gold medal match.
