India has finalised a 20-player badminton contingent for the 20th Asian Games, to be held in Aichi Prefecture and Nagoya, Japan, from September 19 to October 4. The squad features marquee names including PV Sindhu, Lakshya Sen, Satwiksairaj Rankireddy, and Chirag Shetty. Dr P Ankamma Chowdary of the Badminton Association of India has been nominated as the official team delegate for the Games.
