FIFA has launched formal disciplinary proceedings against the Argentine Football Association and several players following violent confrontations with Spanish players after the 2026 World Cup final, which Spain won 1-0. Midfielder Leandro Paredes faces three counts of alleged assault, defender Nahuel Molina two counts, and assistant coach Roberto Ayala one count, while Spain's Gavi also faces an unsporting behaviour charge. The AFA itself faces wider charges covering discriminatory chants, security breaches, late kick-offs, and the display of a politically sensitive 'Las Malvinas son Argentinas' banner after Argentina beat England in the semi-finals.
