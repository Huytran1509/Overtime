The 30th BWF World Championships concluded on 23 August in New Delhi with France claiming a historic double. Thom Gicquel and Delphine Delrue won the mixed doubles gold — France's first ever world title in that event — defeating top seeds Feng Yan Zhe and Huang Dong Ping of China in a gruelling 78-minute three-set battle. Shortly after, 21-year-old Alex Lanier became France's first ever men's singles world champion, dismantling Japan's Kodai Naraoka 21-11, 21-11 in the final.
