The 2026 VALORANT Champions world championship tournament opens its group stage in Shanghai on September 24, just two days away, with 16 teams from the Americas, EMEA, Pacific, and China competing for a $2.25 million prize pool. Matches during the group and early playoff stages will be held at Jing'an Sports Center, before the top four move to Mercedes-Benz Arena for Finals Weekend on October 18. Defending champions NRG enter the field alongside contenders including T1, Paper Rex, EDward Gaming, LOUD, and Karmine Corp.
