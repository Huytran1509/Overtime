Alexandra Eala of the Philippines clinched her first WTA Tour singles title on August 3 by defeating top seed Jessica Pegula 4-6, 6-4, 6-0 in the Mubadala DC Open final in Washington, D.C. The 21-year-old became the first tennis player from the Philippines to win a WTA Tour singles title and is projected to break into the world's top 20 rankings for the first time ahead of the US Open. The final had been suspended overnight by heavy rain and lightning, with Eala coming from behind to dominate the deciding set after play resumed.
