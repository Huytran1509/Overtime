FIFA suspended three Argentina players after violence erupted following their loss to Spain in the 2026 World Cup final. Leandro Paredes received the heaviest punishment — a 10-match ban — for grabbing Eric García by the throat and pushing Gavi, while Nahuel Molina got seven games and Thiago Almada one. The Argentine Football Association was also fined over $110,000 for a range of incidents during the tournament. The ban effectively keeps Paredes out of the Argentina squad for a full year, through June 2027.
