Three-time Grand Slam champion Stan Wawrinka's long career is now one tournament away from ending after he was eliminated from the Lyon Open by top seed Flavio Cobolli 6-2, 6-4 in the second round on Thursday. The 41-year-old Swiss entered Lyon on a wild card and had needed nearly three hours to beat Martin Landaluce in round one. His sole remaining event is the Basel tournament, where he will make his final professional appearance.
