FIFPRO revealed its 2026 Men's World XI, determined by votes from nearly 20,000 professional players across 73 countries, and Ballon d'Or winner Harry Kane was a surprising omission. Kane's exclusion is unprecedented — the first time since the award's creation in 2005 that an active Ballon d'Or winner has failed to make the peer-voted XI. Lionel Messi claimed a record-extending 18th selection, while the attack line featured Haaland, Mbappé, and Lamine Yamal.
