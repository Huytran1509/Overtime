Bayern Munich assistant Aaron Danks confirmed on Friday that head coach Vincent Kompany is on track for a full recovery after collapsing at a reserve team training session on October 23. Kompany was hospitalised and treated for meningeal irritation, an inflammation of the tissue around the brain or spinal cord. Danks has remained in charge for first-team duties, including Saturday's Bundesliga match against Mainz, with an international break then giving Kompany more time to recuperate.
