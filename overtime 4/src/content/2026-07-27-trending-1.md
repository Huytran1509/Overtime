LeBron James, aged 41, announced on July 24 that he is signing a two-year, $8 million contract with the Philadelphia 76ers for his 24th NBA season, calling it his final career decision after seriously considering retirement. James will join Joel Embiid in Philadelphia's frontcourt, and the 76ers also acquired Jaylen Brown from Boston earlier this offseason, giving them two former Finals MVPs alongside Embiid. James said he had concluded he still loved basketball and believed he could help make the Sixers a championship team.
