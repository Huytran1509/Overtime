Scottie Scheffler claimed his second FedEx Cup title in three years after winning the 2026 Tour Championship at East Lake Golf Club in Atlanta, finishing at 16 under and three strokes ahead of Viktor Hovland. In doing so, Scheffler became only the third golfer — alongside Tiger Woods and Rory McIlroy — to win multiple FedEx Cups. The $10 million winner's share pushed Scheffler's career PGA Tour earnings to $130.4 million, surpassing Tiger Woods' long-standing record. It was his 22nd PGA Tour victory and third title of the 2026 season.
