The Nigeria Football Federation dismissed Super Falcons head coach Justine Madugu and his entire technical staff following the team's failure to qualify for the 2027 FIFA Women's World Cup — a first in the country's history since 1991. Nigeria were knocked out in the WAFCON quarterfinals by Cameroon and then lost a playoff to South Africa, triggering an immediate investigation by the NFF into the causes of the collapse. The federation now faces a leadership crisis of its own ahead of scheduled elections in September.
