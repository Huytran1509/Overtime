The League of Legends LCS 2026 Summer Playoffs and LCK Season Playoffs both concluded their final qualification matches around September 13-14, locking in the last regional seeds for the 2026 League of Legends World Championship. Hanwha Life Esports, winners of MSI 2026, secured automatic Worlds qualification, while the LCK's performance at MSI also earned the region's fourth seed a direct entry into the Swiss Stage rather than the Play-in. The World Championship itself is scheduled to begin on October 15, with the grand final to be held at the Barclays Center in Brooklyn on November 14.
