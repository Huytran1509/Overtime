Top-ranked Jannik Sinner confirmed his withdrawal from the 2026 US Open on August 21, citing an ongoing right knee injury that has kept him sidelined since his Wimbledon title defence in July. The Italian, who won his second consecutive Wimbledon crown by beating Alexander Zverev, was forced to miss the hard-court events in Montreal and Cincinnati as well. It is the first Grand Slam Sinner has missed since his professional debut at the 2019 US Open.
