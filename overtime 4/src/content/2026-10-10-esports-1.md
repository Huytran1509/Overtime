The Play-In Stage draw for the 2026 League of Legends World Championship took place today following the conclusion of the CBLOL Split 2 Grand Finals between LOS and FURIA, which determined Brazil's second seed. The four teams entering the Play-Ins — Karmine Corp, Cloud9, MVK Esports, and the CBLOL runner-up — were randomly drawn into a best-of-five double-elimination bracket. The Play-In Stage itself runs from October 15 to 18, with the sole survivor joining the 15 other teams in the Swiss Stage.
