Paris Saint-Germain have completed the signing of Belgian winger Mika Godts from Ajax for a total package of €55 million. The 21-year-old, who scored 17 goals and provided 15 assists in 44 matches last season, has signed a contract with PSG until 2031 and will wear the number 22 shirt. Ajax originally demanded €60 million but ultimately accepted the lower fee, representing a remarkable return on the €1 million they paid to sign Godts from KRC Genk three years ago.
