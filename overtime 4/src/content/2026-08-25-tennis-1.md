Top-ranked Jannik Sinner announced on August 21 that he will miss the 2026 US Open due to a right knee injury that has sidelined him since he successfully defended his Wimbledon title against Alexander Zverev in July. The Italian, who had already been forced to skip the Masters 1000 events in Canada and Cincinnati, underwent tests in Monte Carlo that confirmed he needs more recovery time. It is the first Grand Slam Sinner has missed since his major debut in 2019, and he remains comfortably at the top of the ATP rankings despite the setback.
