South Korea's An Se Young claimed her second women's singles world title in New Delhi, beating Japan's defending champion Yamaguchi Akane 21-17, 21-14 in a dominant straight-game final. An did not drop a single game throughout the entire tournament, capping a remarkable season in which she had already won seven titles from nine tournaments before the World Championships. The result denied Yamaguchi, a three-time world champion, back-to-back titles.
