Defending champion Aryna Sabalenka kept alive her bid for a historic third consecutive US Open title by beating sixth seed Linda Noskova 7-6, 3-6, 7-6 in a tightly contested quarterfinal at Arthur Ashe Stadium. Sabalenka, who has won the title in 2024 and 2025, now advances to the semifinals where she continues to be the tournament favourite. Carlos Alcaraz and Ben Shelton are set to contest the other men's quarterfinal.
