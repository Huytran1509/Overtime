Carlos Alcaraz claimed his maiden Paris Masters title on Sunday, beating American Ben Shelton 7-6 (5), 7-5 in the final at the Plenitude Arena in Nanterre. The win was Alcaraz's fifth title of 2026 and his ninth ATP Masters 1000 crown, avenging a US Open quarter-final defeat to Shelton earlier in the season. He now heads into the ATP Finals in Turin on a nine-match winning streak after back-to-back titles in Vienna and Paris.
