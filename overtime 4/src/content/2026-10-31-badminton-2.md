Indian para-badminton player Nitesh Kumar returned from the 2026 Asian Para Games in Aichi-Nagoya, Japan, having won a gold and a silver medal. Kumar credited his father, Vijender Singh, as his strongest source of support since a career-defining accident in 2009 set him on the path to professional para-sport. India concluded its historic campaign at the Games by finishing fourth overall with a record-breaking 114 medals.
