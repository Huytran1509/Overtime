Badminton at the 2026 Asian Games will be held from September 20 to 29 at the Ichinomiya City Municipal Gymnasium in Aichi Prefecture, Japan. The event marks the sport's seventeenth consecutive appearance at the Asian Games. Fresh off their China Masters triumph, India's Satwik-Chirag will look to defend the men's doubles gold they won at the previous edition of the continental games.
