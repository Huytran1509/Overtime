The 2026 Leagues Cup, pitting 18 MLS clubs against all 18 Liga MX sides, kicked off its league phase on August 4 and runs through August 13. For the first time in tournament history, selected Phase One matches are being played in Mexico, adding a new dimension to the competition. August 6 fixtures include clashes between New York City FC vs Santos Laguna, Chicago Fire vs Necaxa, and Club América vs San Diego FC. The top four teams from each league will advance to the knockout stage, with the three finishers earning berths in the 2027 CONCACAF Champions Cup.
