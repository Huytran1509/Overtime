India's women's cricket team takes on Sri Lanka in the gold medal final at the 2026 Asian Games on September 22 in Nisshin, Japan, a rematch of the Hangzhou 2023 final which India won. India stormed into the final with a 114-run demolition of Bangladesh in the semi-final, powered by Shafali Verma's maiden T20I century off just 49 balls. Smriti Mandhana also made history in that match, overtaking Suzie Bates to become the leading run-scorer in women's T20I cricket.
