New Delhi is confirmed as the host city for the 2026 Badminton World Championships, marking India's return to hosting the event for the first time in 17 years, since Hyderabad in 2009. The BWF made the announcement following the conclusion of the 2025 World Championships in Paris, with the competition also returning to Asia for the first time since Nanjing in 2018. India has a strong record at the championships, with PV Sindhu being the country's most decorated competitor.
