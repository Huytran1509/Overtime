Real Madrid forward Kylian Mbappé made history on October 14 by becoming the youngest player ever to reach 100 UEFA Champions League appearances, achieving the milestone at age 27 years and 298 days during Madrid's dramatic 4-3 win at Roma. He surpassed Lionel Messi's previous record by almost ten months, and celebrated by scoring a 25th-minute equaliser. Across his 100 appearances, Mbappé has now scored 72 goals, moving him into fifth place on the competition's all-time scoring chart ahead of Raúl.
