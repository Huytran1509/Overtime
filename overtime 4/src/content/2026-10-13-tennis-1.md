Alexander Zverev is mathematically guaranteed to finish the 2026 ATP season as year-end world number one, following Carlos Alcaraz's shock third-round defeat to Belgium's Zizou Bergs at the Shanghai Masters. Zverev, who won both the French Open and the US Open this year, will become the first German man to hold the year-end top ranking and only the 30th player in ATP history to do so. World number one Jannik Sinner, who earlier shut down his season due to a knee injury, will cede his ranking position at the end of the year.
