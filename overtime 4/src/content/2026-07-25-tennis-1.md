The USTA released the official entry lists for the 2026 US Open, with two-time defending champion Aryna Sabalenka and Wimbledon winner Jannik Sinner headlining the women's and men's draws respectively. Sabalenka will attempt to become the first woman to win three consecutive US Open titles since Serena Williams achieved the feat from 2012 to 2014. Sinner arrives in form having recently claimed his fifth major crown at Wimbledon, while Carlos Alcaraz, who has not played since April due to a wrist injury, is on the entry list though his fitness remains uncertain.
