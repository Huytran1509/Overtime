Stan Wawrinka, the 41-year-old three-time Grand Slam champion, plays his opening match on Tuesday against eighth seed Jiri Lehecka at the Swiss Indoors Basel, the final tournament of his professional career. Novak Djokovic has entered on a wildcard, making his first appearance at the event since he won it in 2011, with the Serb seeking to boost his ATP Finals qualification bid. Ben Shelton is top seed in a star-studded field that also includes Arthur Fils, Taylor Fritz and Rafael Jodar.
