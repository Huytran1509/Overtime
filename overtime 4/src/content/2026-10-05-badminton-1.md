The 26th edition of the BWF World Junior Championships got underway in Cairo, Egypt on October 5, running through to October 17. The event was relocated from Surakarta, Indonesia after the Indonesian association withdrew due to internal considerations, with Egypt being confirmed as the new host in May 2026. The tournament's team competition, the Suhandinata Cup, runs from October 5 to 10 at the Cairo Stadium Indoor Halls Complex.
