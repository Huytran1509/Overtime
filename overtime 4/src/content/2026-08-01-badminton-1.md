Two young Indian shuttlers, Unnati Hooda and Tanvi Sharma, advanced to the semi-finals of the 2026 BWF Super 300 Taipei Open on Friday, raising hopes of an all-Indian final. Hooda, ranked 28th in the world, defeated compatriot Devika Sihag 23-21, 21-16 in an all-Indian quarter-final, while Sharma came from behind to beat Thailand's Supanida Katethong 21-15, 18-21, 21-14. The tournament is being held at Taipei Arena and runs until August 2.
