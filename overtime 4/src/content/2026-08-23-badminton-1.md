The 30th edition of the BWF World Championships, held at Indira Gandhi Arena in New Delhi, reached its penultimate stage with all five disciplines completing their semi-finals on Saturday. India is hosting the tournament for the first time since 2009, adding extra significance to the occasion for the home crowd. The finals across men's singles, women's singles, men's doubles, women's doubles, and mixed doubles are scheduled for Sunday 23 August.
