Novak Djokovic suffered an early exit at the 2026 Cincinnati Open, falling to Argentine qualifier Thiago Agustin Tirante 2-6, 6-4, 6-4 in a match lasting nearly three hours in humid Ohio conditions. The 24-time Grand Slam champion had spoken of struggles with his body during the match. The Cincinnati Open, running August 13–23, is the last major warm-up event before the US Open, and is missing both World No. 1 Jannik Sinner and 2025 champion Carlos Alcaraz due to injury.
