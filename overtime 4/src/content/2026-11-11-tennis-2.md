World number one Jannik Sinner addressed the media at a Milan event on November 10, confirming that his primary comeback target is the 2027 Australian Open following a prolonged right knee injury. The 25-year-old has been sidelined since winning his fifth Grand Slam title at Wimbledon in July, missing the US Open and the entire Asian swing. Sinner indicated he may use the rescheduled Six Kings Slam exhibition event in Saudi Arabia in late December as a warm-up ahead of Melbourne.
