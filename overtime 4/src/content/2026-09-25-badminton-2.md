India's men's badminton team guaranteed themselves a medal at the 2026 Asian Games after defeating host nation Japan in the quarter-finals at Ichinomiya City Municipal Gymnasium. Lakshya Sen led the charge with a hard-fought three-game victory over world number seven Kodai Naraoka, and India advanced to the semi-finals to secure at least a bronze. The result improves on their silver medal finish at the Hangzhou 2023 Asian Games.
