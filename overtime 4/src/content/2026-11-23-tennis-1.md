Carlos Alcaraz won the ATP Finals for the first time in his career, defeating Alexander Zverev 6-2, 7-6(6) in the Turin final to claim his 30th career title and sixth of the season. The win came despite Alcaraz facing three consecutive set points in the second-set tie-break, with the Spaniard ultimately holding his nerve to triumph. He also becomes only the third Spaniard in history to lift the ATP Finals trophy, joining Manuel Orantes (1976) and Alex Corretja (1998), and took home a record champion's prize of $5,171,000.
