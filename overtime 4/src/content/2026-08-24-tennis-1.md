Jannik Sinner confirmed he will miss the 2026 US Open due to a persistent right knee problem, marking the first Grand Slam he has skipped since his debut at the 2019 edition. The Italian, who successfully defended his Wimbledon title in July, has been sidelined ever since and was unable to recover in time for the 30 August main draw at Flushing Meadows. His absence opens the door for Carlos Alcaraz, who announced his return from a four-month injury layoff just one day earlier and is now the favourite to retain his title.
