The 30th edition of the BWF World Championships will be held at the Indira Gandhi Arena in New Delhi from August 17 to 23, marking the first time India has hosted the tournament since the 2009 edition in Hyderabad. The event is generating significant domestic anticipation, with Indian players including PV Sindhu, Lakshya Sen, and the doubles pairing of Satwiksairaj Rankireddy and Chirag Shetty expected to compete. India last produced a World Championships gold medallist through Sindhu, who has amassed five medals at the event in total.
