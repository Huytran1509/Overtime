Badminton at the 2026 Asian Games is scheduled to take place from September 20 to 29 at Ichinomiya City Municipal Gymnasium in Aichi Prefecture, Japan. This will mark the sport's seventeenth consecutive appearance as an official discipline at the Asian Games, with top nations including South Korea, China, Indonesia, India, and Japan set to compete. An Se-young has already confirmed her participation as she defends her Asian Games women's singles title.
