The WTA 250 Chennai Open women's singles final between Indonesia's Janice Tjen and Poland's Magda Linette was postponed from its scheduled Sunday slot due to rain and poor air quality linked to Diwali fireworks smoke. The match was rescheduled to Monday, November 9, at 11 a.m. local time. The incident drew criticism of local organizers and was compounded by reports that the tournament will move to Manila from next year.
