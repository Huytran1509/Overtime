Bayern Munich striker Harry Kane claimed the 2026 Ballon d'Or at a ceremony held at the London Palladium, beating Barcelona's Lamine Yamal and Real Madrid's Kylian Mbappe. Kane became the first English winner of the award since Michael Owen in 2001, and only the fifth Englishman ever to lift the prize. The ceremony was held outside France for the first time in the award's history, with London chosen to honour Stanley Matthews, winner of the inaugural Ballon d'Or in 1956. On the women's side, Alexia Putellas claimed a historic third consecutive award.
