The 30th BWF World Championships are set to take place at Indira Gandhi Arena in New Delhi, India, from 17 to 23 August 2026. This marks the first time India has hosted the prestigious event since the 2009 edition in Hyderabad. The tournament, coming just weeks after the China Open, will see Yamaguchi Akane defend her women's singles title among a field of the world's top-ranked players.
