Belgium's Elise Mertens beat sixth-seeded Iga Swiatek 7-6, 6-3 to advance to the semi-finals of the China Open in Beijing, where she will face home favourite Zheng Qinwen. The run marks Mertens' first WTA 1000 singles semi-final since 2021 and includes wins over three Grand Slam champions in Jelena Ostapenko, Coco Gauff, and now Swiatek. Mertens needed one hour and fifty minutes to dispatch the six-time Grand Slam winner.
