FIFA handed down bans to three Argentina players stemming from violent conduct immediately after the 2026 World Cup final loss to Spain. Midfielder Leandro Paredes received the harshest punishment — a 10-match suspension — after grabbing Spain's Eric Garcia by the throat and shoving Gavi to the ground. Defender Nahuel Molina was banned for seven games, while Thiago Almada received a one-match ban. The Argentine Football Association was also fined $110,000 for a range of additional tournament-related violations, including a political banner displayed after the semi-final and discriminatory fan chants.
