Rafael Leao confirmed his seven-year stint at AC Milan is over, with the Portuguese winger set to join Turkish champions Galatasaray in a deal worth approximately €45 million. The 27-year-old scored 80 goals in 291 appearances for the Rossoneri and was a key figure in their 2021-22 Serie A title win. Leao expressed emotion at his departure, saying he had wanted to say goodbye on the pitch but was unable to feature in Milan's 2-0 win over Venezia before flying to Istanbul for his medical.
