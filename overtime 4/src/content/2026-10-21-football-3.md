Cristiano Ronaldo has been officially handed a 30-day suspension and a 255-euro fine by the Portuguese Football Federation following his walkout from the national team camp in September. The 41-year-old left the squad ahead of Portugal's Nations League match against Denmark after accusing coach Jorge Jesus of breaking an agreement over his playing time. Since the suspension is retroactive to October 1, Ronaldo is expected to be available for Portugal's next Nations League match on November 14.
