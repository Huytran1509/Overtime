South Korea's An Se-young set a new all-time record for the most singles titles in women's badminton history by claiming her 47th career international title at the Denmark Open, according to the Badminton World Federation. She overcame an ankle injury to defeat world No. 3 Akane Yamaguchi of Japan in the final, claiming her ninth gold medal of the season. Boasting a remarkable win rate of over 98 percent, An is already targeting a tenth title of the year at the upcoming French Open.
