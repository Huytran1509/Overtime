The League of Legends Championship Series staged its first-ever Homegrounds event in Mexico City on August 29-30, hosted by LYON at the Arena Esports Stadium at Artz Pedregal. The live event featured four teams — LYON, Cloud9, Team Liquid, and Shopify Rebellion — competing in front of a local crowd as part of Week 6 of the LCS Summer Split. The initiative is designed to bring the LCS closer to fans across the Americas, with the league's championship weekend set for Atlanta in October.
