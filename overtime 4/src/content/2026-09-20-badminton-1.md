Badminton competition at the 2026 Asian Games officially got underway on September 20 at the Ichinomiya City Municipal Gymnasium in Aichi Prefecture, Japan. India's women's team, led by two-time Olympic medallist PV Sindhu, opened their campaign against Kazakhstan in the round of 16. A 20-member Indian squad is competing across all seven categories, with team event finals scheduled for September 24 and individual events running from September 25 to 29.
