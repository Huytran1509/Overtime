India's two leading singles players, PV Sindhu and Lakshya Sen, were both eliminated in the opening round of the Denmark Open 2026 BWF Super 750 event in Odense. Sindhu, ranked 10th in the world, lost 13-21, 25-23, 21-19 to Germany's Yvonne Li, ranked 50th, in a major upset — her first loss in four career meetings against Li. Lakshya Sen went down to Chinese Taipei's Chou Tien Chen 18-21, 21-13, 15-21 in a 63-minute contest. Kidambi Srikanth and Tanvi Sharma did advance to the second round.
