India's Lakshya Sen saw his impressive 13-match winning streak come to an end after losing to Denmark's world number three Anders Antonsen 21-13, 21-13 in the men's singles semi-finals of the BWF Super 500 Hong Kong Open. The Indian doubles pair of Satwiksairaj Rankireddy and Chirag Shetty also fell at the semi-final stage, ending India's campaign. Despite the defeat, Sen secured his place at the season-ending BWF World Tour Finals in Hangzhou, scheduled for December 9-13.
