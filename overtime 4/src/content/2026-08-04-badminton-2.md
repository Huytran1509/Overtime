The Philippine Badminton Association launched the Philippine Junior International Challenge on August 4 at Gameville Central Park in Mandaluyong, drawing players from around 32 countries including powerhouses Malaysia, Indonesia, and India. The under-19 and under-15 event is the third international competition hosted by the PBAD in quick succession following the Philippine Open in June and the Philippine International Challenge days earlier. Officials expressed optimism that the tournament will help uncover new Filipino badminton talent.
