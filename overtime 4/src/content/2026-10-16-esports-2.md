The decision affects all three FIFAe World Cup competitions, covering eFootball on console and mobile as well as Rocket League, following an assessment of regional security developments. Konami also confirmed the postponement of the FIFAe World Cup 2026 through an official eFootball announcement, with a rescheduled date yet to be announced.
