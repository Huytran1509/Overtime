The 30th edition of the BWF World Championships is set to take place at Indira Gandhi Arena in New Delhi, India, from August 17 to 23, 2026. This will be the first time India has hosted the event since 2009 and marks the championships' return to Asia after eight years. India's top stars, including PV Sindhu and Lakshya Sen, are expected to lead the home contingent, with all five disciplines — men's and women's singles, men's and women's doubles, and mixed doubles — contested.
