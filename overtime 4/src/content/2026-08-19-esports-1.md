The Counter-Strike 2 playoffs at the Esports World Cup 2026 kicked off on August 19 in Paris, with 16 teams advancing from the group stage for a single-elimination bracket. The tournament features 32 of the world's best teams competing for a $2 million prize pool, the largest CS2 purse in EWC history. Defending champions The MongolZ, along with powerhouses Falcons, FURIA, G2, and MOUZ, are among the key contenders. The grand final is set for August 23 at the Accor Arena.
