World number one Jannik Sinner and 24-time Grand Slam champion Novak Djokovic have pulled out of the ATP Masters 1000 National Bank Open in Montreal, which begins August 1. Sinner, who successfully defended his Wimbledon title earlier this month, cited health reasons, while no specific injury was mentioned by Djokovic. Carlos Alcaraz had already been excluded from the entry list due to a wrist injury, meaning all three of men's tennis' biggest draws will miss the event. Tournament director Valérie Tétreault expressed strong frustration and confirmed Tennis Canada is in talks with the ATP over the recurring issue of top-player withdrawals.
