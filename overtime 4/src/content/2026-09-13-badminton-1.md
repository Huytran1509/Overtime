India's Satwiksairaj Rankireddy and Chirag Shetty made history by becoming the first Indian players ever to win the BWF Super 750 China Masters title. The duo overcame China's He Jiting and Ren Xiangyu 11-21, 21-13, 21-17 in a 70-minute final at Shenzhen Arena, staging a comeback after losing the opening game. The victory was their 10th career BWF World Tour title and second of the 2026 season, ending two previous runner-up finishes at the tournament. The pair now head to Japan as reigning Asian Games champions with strong momentum ahead of the 2026 Asian Games starting September 20.
