Badminton at the 2026 Asian Games in Aichi-Nagoya, Japan is set to get underway from September 20 to 29 at the Ichinomiya City Municipal Gymnasium. India will be represented by PV Sindhu, Lakshya Sen, and the freshly-crowned China Masters champions Satwik and Chirag, among others. World champion An Se-young of South Korea is also confirmed to compete as she looks to defend her Asian Games title. The event marks the 17th consecutive appearance of badminton as an official sport at the Asian Games.
