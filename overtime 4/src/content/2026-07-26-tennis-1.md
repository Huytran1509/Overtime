The USTA released the direct entry lists for the 2026 US Open, with two-time defending champion Aryna Sabalenka and Wimbledon winner Jannik Sinner leading their respective draws. Sabalenka is chasing a historic third consecutive US Open title, a feat not achieved since Serena Williams in 2012-2014. Sinner, fresh off his fifth Grand Slam title at Wimbledon, is looking to reclaim the US Open crown he lost to Carlos Alcaraz last year, with the main draw beginning August 30.
