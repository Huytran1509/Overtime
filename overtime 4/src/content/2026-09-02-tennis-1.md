Novak Djokovic, 39, suffered a historic first-round defeat to Argentina's Mariano Navone at the 2026 US Open, losing 7-6, 5-7, 4-6, 6-2, 6-1 in a four-hour-and-36-minute marathon. The loss ended Djokovic's streak of 78 consecutive opening-round victories at Grand Slams — the longest such streak in the Open era — and marked his earliest Grand Slam exit since the 2006 Australian Open. As a result, Djokovic will drop out of the ATP Top 10 for the first time since 2018, and this is the first Slam since 2003 not to feature Djokovic, Federer, or Nadal in the second round.
