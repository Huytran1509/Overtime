The 2026 Leagues Cup began on August 4 with six matches on opening night, five of which were first-ever Leagues Cup meetings between MLS and Liga MX clubs. The tournament features 36 clubs — all 18 Liga MX teams and 18 qualified MLS sides — competing across North America through September 6. For the first time in the competition's history, matches will also be played on Mexican soil, at the home venues of the top-ranked Liga MX clubs.
