Paris Saint-Germain claimed the UEFA Super Cup for the second consecutive year with a 2-1 win over Aston Villa in Salzburg on August 12. Goals from Khvicha Kvaratskhelia and Désiré Doué — the latter confirmed by VAR — sealed the victory, with Villa's 17-year-old debutant Brian Madjo scoring a consolation volley. PSG became only the third club in history to win back-to-back UEFA Super Cups.
