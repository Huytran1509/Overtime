India's Satwiksairaj Rankireddy and Chirag Shetty claimed their maiden China Masters title in Shenzhen, rallying from a first-game drubbing to defeat China's He Jiting and Ren Xiangyu 11-21, 21-13, 21-17 in the BWF Super 750 final. The triumph made them the first Indian badminton players to win the China Masters since the tournament began in 2005. It is also their 10th career title on the BWF World Tour and their second of the 2026 season, providing timely confidence ahead of the Asian Games later in September.
