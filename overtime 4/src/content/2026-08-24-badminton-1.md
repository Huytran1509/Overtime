Alex Lanier became the first French player ever to win the men's singles title at the BWF World Championships, demolishing Japan's Kodai Naraoka 21-11, 21-11 in a dominant 50-minute final in New Delhi on 23 August. Lanier, seeded ninth and the 2025 European champion, dropped only two games across the entire tournament. France also claimed the mixed doubles gold through Thom Gicquel and Delphine Delrue, making France only the second European nation to win more than one gold at a single world championships edition.
