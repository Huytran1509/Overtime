Cristiano Ronaldo announced his retirement from international football on November 5, bringing an end to a 23-year career with Portugal. He leaves as his country's all-time record scorer and most-capped player, having netted 146 goals across 234 appearances. The retirement followed a public rift with new Portugal coach Jorge Jesus, who kept Ronaldo as an unused substitute in a Nations League match in October. Ronaldo will continue playing at club level with Al-Nassr.
