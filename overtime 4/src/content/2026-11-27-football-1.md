FIFA's Extraordinary Congress confirmed the United States, Mexico, Costa Rica, and Jamaica as co-hosts of the 2031 FIFA Women's World Cup — the first edition to feature 48 teams. England, Northern Ireland, Scotland, and Wales were simultaneously awarded the 2035 edition, marking England's first time hosting the women's tournament.
