FIFA suspended three Argentina players for physical altercations with Spain opponents moments after losing the 2026 World Cup final. Midfielder Leandro Paredes received the stiffest punishment — a 10-match ban — after grabbing Spain's Eric Garcia by the throat and pushing Gavi to the ground, a sanction ranked among the harshest in World Cup history. Defender Nahuel Molina was banned for seven games and Thiago Almada for one, with fines of $90,000 and $30,000 imposed respectively. The ban effectively rules Paredes out of Argentina duty for a full year, covering all 10 scheduled national-team fixtures through June 2027.
