The 2026 League of Legends World Championship, held across the United States, completed its Play-In stage at Riot Games Arena in Los Angeles and moves into the Swiss Stage at the Credit Union of Texas Event Center in Allen, starting October 23. The tournament carries a five-million-dollar prize pool, with the champion taking home one million dollars. South Korean organisation Hanwha Life Esports, winners of the 2026 Mid-Season Invitational, are among the favourites, while the Grand Final is set for November 14 at the Barclays Center in Brooklyn, New York.
