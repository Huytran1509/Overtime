China's Wang Zhiyi advanced to the women's singles final of the Hong Kong Open for the seventh time this season, defeating Japan's Tomoka Miyazaki in straight sets. With world number one An Se-young absent from the tournament, Wang faces Thailand's Supanida Chochuwong in Sunday's final — Chochuwong's first Hong Kong Open final appearance after beating compatriot Ratchanok Intanon in the semi-finals. Wang's only title this year came at the All England Open, where she previously defeated An Se-young.
