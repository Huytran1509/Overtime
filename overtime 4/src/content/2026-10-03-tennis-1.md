Team Europe won the 2026 Laver Cup at The O2 in London, defeating Team World 13-5. Alexander Zverev sealed the title by beating Learner Tien 7-6(3), 6-3 in the final singles match on Sunday, September 27. Team World had led 5-3 midway through Saturday before Europe won four consecutive matches to clinch the trophy. It was captain Yannick Noah's first Laver Cup title, and Europe's sixth overall in nine editions.
