India heads into its first home BWF World Championships since 2009 with genuine medal ambitions across multiple disciplines. Head coach Pullela Gopichand expressed confidence in the Indian contingent, citing PV Sindhu's recent Japan Open title and the in-form men's doubles pair of Satwik-Chirag as key medal contenders. The 30th edition of the tournament in New Delhi marks India's return as hosts after 17 years and is being seen as a defining moment for Indian badminton. Lakshya Sen, 14th seed, begins against Austria's Collins Valentine Filimon.
