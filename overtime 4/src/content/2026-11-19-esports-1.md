The Korea e-Sports Association announced on November 19 that it will host the 2026 Esports Community Games Playoffs on November 21 and 22 in Daejeon, South Korea. A total of 114 participants will compete at Perfect PC Bang in Yuseong to determine the champion teams across multiple titles. The event reflects KeSPA's continued push to broaden grassroots esports competition in the country.
