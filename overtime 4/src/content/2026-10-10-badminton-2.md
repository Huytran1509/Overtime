Two-time Olympic medallist PV Sindhu was eliminated from the BWF Arctic Open 2026 after losing a three-game thriller to world number five Ratchanok Intanon of Thailand in the women's singles quarter-finals. Sindhu lost 21-11, 19-21, 21-18 in a closely contested decider, extending her head-to-head losing streak against the Thai to six consecutive defeats. India's men's doubles pair of Krishna Prasad Garaga and Pruthvi Roy also fell in the quarter-finals, ending India's campaign at the Super 500 event.
