The 30th edition of the BWF World Championships is in full swing at Indira Gandhi Arena in New Delhi, with the second round taking place on August 19. India is hosting the event for the first time since 2009, with 64 men and 64 women competing across singles disciplines, plus 48 pairs across three doubles events. Top seeds An Seyoung of South Korea and Shi Yuqi of China are the ones to beat in women's and men's singles respectively.
