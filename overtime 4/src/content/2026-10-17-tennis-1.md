Spain's 20-year-old Rafael Jodar made history by becoming the youngest player to reach a Shanghai Masters semifinal, defeating Casper Ruud 4-6, 6-4, 6-3 in the quarterfinals. It was Jodar's fourth consecutive comeback win from losing the opening set at the ATP Masters 1000 event. He faces former champion Daniil Medvedev in the last four on October 17, while top seed Alexander Zverev meets Alejandro Davidovich Fokina in the other semifinal.
