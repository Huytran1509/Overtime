The victory ends Sabalenka's run as world No. 1, with Rybakina set to take the top WTA ranking on Monday after the Belarusian had held the position for 99 consecutive weeks. Rybakina broke Sabalenka twice in the deciding third set, dropping just three points on serve during that set for a dominant display. The win also marked Rybakina's 10th career victory over a world No. 1 player, six of those coming against Sabalenka.
