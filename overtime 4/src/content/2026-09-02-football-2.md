FIFA's Disciplinary Committee has imposed formal sanctions on Argentina players and officials for violent conduct following the 2026 World Cup final loss to Spain. Leandro Paredes received a ten-match ban and a $90,000 fine, Nahuel Molina a seven-match ban and a $90,000 fine, and assistant coach Roberto Ayala a three-match ban and a $30,000 fine. The Argentine Football Association was also fined $321,000 and ordered to play its next two home matches at 50% capacity for discriminatory fan behaviour and other breaches.
