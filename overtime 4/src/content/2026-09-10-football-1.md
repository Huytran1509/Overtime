Lionel Messi announced his retirement from international football on August 31, ending 21 years of service to the Argentina national team. The 39-year-old leaves as Argentina's all-time leading scorer and most-capped player, with 125 goals and 68 assists in 207 appearances. His retirement followed Argentina's narrow 1-0 extra-time defeat to Spain in the 2026 FIFA World Cup final, and came weeks after the death of his father Jorge. He will continue playing at club level for Inter Miami in MLS.
