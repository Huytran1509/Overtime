The esports competition at the 2026 Asian Games is progressing at the Aichi International Exhibition Center in Tokoname, with events running from September 23 through October 2. Thirteen video game titles across eleven events are being contested, including eFootball, Honor of Kings, Naraka: Bladepoint, Gran Turismo 7, and Puyo Puyo Champions. The LCK schedule was notably shortened earlier this season specifically to allow South Korean national esports teams to prepare for these Games.
