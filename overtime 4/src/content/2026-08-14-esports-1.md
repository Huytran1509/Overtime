Sixteen of the world's top Rocket League teams are competing at the 2026 Esports World Cup in Paris from 12 to 16 August, with a $1 million prize pool on the line. The event is part of the broader EWC 2026 series, which runs from 6 July to 23 August and features 25 events across 24 esports titles. The tournament relocated to Paris this year due to regional circumstances affecting the usual Saudi Arabian venue.
