Mauricio Pochettino has been confirmed as Manchester United's new head coach, replacing Michael Carrick who was sacked after United's worst start to a Premier League season in 40 years, leaving them in the relegation zone. Pochettino signed a two-and-a-half-year deal taking him through to summer 2029. He will first complete his remaining USMNT duties, including overseeing the USA's CONCACAF Nations League match against Haiti, before crossing to Manchester. His first assignment at United is a trip to Anfield to face Liverpool on November 22.
