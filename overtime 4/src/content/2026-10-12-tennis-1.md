Wildcard entrant Zheng Qinwen made history on October 11 by defeating Mirra Andreeva 7-5, 6-4 in the China Open final in Beijing, becoming the first Chinese woman to win the event since it joined the tour in 2004. Ranked just 54th in the world and competing as a wildcard after a lengthy absence following elbow surgery, the 2024 Paris Olympic champion claimed her first-ever WTA 1000 title and sixth overall. The victory will propel her back into the world top 30.
