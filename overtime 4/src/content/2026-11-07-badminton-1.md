Muhammad Ali Larosh and Mahoor Shahzad of Pakistan WAPDA clinched the men's and women's singles titles respectively at the national badminton championships in Islamabad on November 7. Larosh defeated Shoaib Riaz of Pakistan Army in straight games, while Shahzad overcame Ammarah Ishtiaq in a hard-fought three-game women's final. Pakistan Army players partially compensated by claiming victories in the women's and mixed doubles events.
