The esports tournament at the 2026 Asian Games officially began on September 23 and runs through October 2 at the Aichi International Exhibition Center in Tokoname, Japan. A total of 23 nations are competing across multiple game titles in what is a landmark moment for competitive gaming at the continental multi-sport level. The Olympic Council of Asia, together with the Japan Esports Union and the Asian Electronic Sports Federation, are co-organising the event.
