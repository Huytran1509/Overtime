The Italian now leads teammate George Russell by 123 points in the drivers' championship and needs just a ninth-place finish at the Mexico City Grand Prix next week to be crowned champion. If successful, he would become the youngest driver to win the F1 title and the first Italian champion since Alberto Ascari in 1953.
