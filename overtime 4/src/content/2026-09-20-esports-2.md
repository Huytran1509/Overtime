The esports tournament at the 2026 Asian Games is scheduled to run from September 23 to October 2 at the Aichi International Exhibition Center in Tokoname, Japan, with 23 nations competing across 13 titles in 11 events. The lineup was finalised in February 2025 following approval by the Olympic Council of Asia's executive board, which includes titles such as Honor of Kings, Mobile Legends: Bang Bang, and Gran Turismo 7. The competition forms a key part of a growing push to integrate esports into major multi-sport events in Asia.
