India's para-badminton contingent from the Asian Para Games 2026 met Prime Minister Narendra Modi on November 6, with athletes describing the interaction as motivating ahead of the 2028 Los Angeles Paralympics. Nitesh Kumar claimed gold in men's singles SL3 by defeating compatriot Pramod Bhagat in the final, while Thulasimathi Murugesan and Manasi Joshi won the women's doubles SL3-SU5 title. India finished the Games with an all-time best haul of 114 medals across para-sports.
