Japan's world champion Akane Yamaguchi and Chinese Taipei's veteran Chou Tien-chen won their respective singles titles at the BWF China Open 2026 Super 1000 event in Changzhou. Chou defeated France's Toma Junior Popov 21-15, 7-21, 21-13 in a hard-fought three-game final to claim his first Super 1000 title since 2019. Yamaguchi beat home favourite Chen Yufei in just 44 minutes to secure her second China Open women's singles crown, having also defeated Chen in the 2026 Thailand Open final.
