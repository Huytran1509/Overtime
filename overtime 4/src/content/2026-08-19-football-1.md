Barcelona have officially signed Spain captain Rodri from Manchester City in a deal worth an initial €60 million, with up to €16.5 million more in add-ons. The 30-year-old, who won the Golden Ball at the 2026 World Cup and led Spain to its second World Cup title, signed a four-year contract and turned down interest from Real Madrid. He was unveiled at Camp Nou on August 18, where he described the move as "a new, important chapter" in his career. At Barcelona, Rodri reunites with seven of his World Cup-winning Spain teammates.
