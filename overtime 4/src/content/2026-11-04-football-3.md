Spanish second-division footballer Jesus de Miguel, 30, died on Tuesday after suffering a cardiac arrest following a violent collision with FC Andorra goalkeeper Jesus Owono during a match the previous day. De Miguel was rushed to hospital after being left unconscious on the pitch, but his neurological condition worsened overnight and he was pronounced dead at 8:00 am. Spanish police opened an involuntary manslaughter investigation, and the Royal Spanish Football Federation suspended all Copa del Rey and Copa de la Reina fixtures as a mark of respect.
