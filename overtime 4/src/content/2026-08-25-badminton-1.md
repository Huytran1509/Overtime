The final day of the 2026 BWF World Championships in New Delhi produced a landmark moment for French badminton, as 21-year-old Alex Lanier demolished Japan's Naraoka Kodai 21-11, 21-11 to become the first Frenchman ever to win the men's singles world title. The mixed doubles pair of Thom Gicquel and Delphine Delrue had earlier upset top seeds Feng Yanzhe and Huang Dongping of China to claim France's first-ever mixed doubles world gold, making France only the second European nation to win more than one gold in a single edition of the championships.
