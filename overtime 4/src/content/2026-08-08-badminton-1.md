Japan's reigning world champion Yamaguchi Akane defeated home favourite Chen Yufei to claim the women's singles crown at the BWF Super 1000 China Open in Changzhou. The China Open results carry major significance for world ranking points ahead of the upcoming BWF World Championships in New Delhi.
