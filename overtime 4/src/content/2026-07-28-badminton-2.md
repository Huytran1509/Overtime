PV Sindhu suffered a heartbreaking defeat at the BWF Super 1000 China Open in Changzhou, losing to world No. 4 Chen Yufei in three games after holding four match points at 20-16 in the second set. Chen Yufei saved all four and went on to win the decider, ending Sindhu's hopes of back-to-back wins over her Chinese rival following her Japan Open title the previous week. The loss also saw India's other contenders, Lakshya Sen and Ayush Shetty, exit the tournament on the same day, ending India's campaign.
