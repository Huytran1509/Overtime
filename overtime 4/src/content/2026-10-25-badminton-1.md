Two-time Olympic medallist PV Sindhu advanced to the women's singles final of the BWF 750 French Open in Paris after defeating Japan's Hina Akechi 21-10, 20-22, 21-16 in a hard-fought 73-minute contest. Sindhu will face reigning Olympic and world champion An Se Young of South Korea in Sunday's title match, a player she has never beaten in ten previous tour meetings.
