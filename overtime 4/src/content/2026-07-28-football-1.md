Norway's Football Federation president Lise Klaveness has confirmed plans to lodge a formal complaint with FIFA's ethics committee over the suspension of Folarin Balogun's red card ban during the 2026 World Cup. Balogun received a straight red card against Bosnia and Herzegovina but was allowed to play in the Round of 16 against Belgium after US President Donald Trump intervened by phoning FIFA president Gianni Infantino, who then suspended the ban. Norway intends to merge the Balogun complaint with an existing grievance over Infantino's decision to award FIFA's inaugural Peace Prize to Trump. The NFF board is set to formally vote on the matter at a meeting on August 6.
