The world number five duo rallied from a first-game deficit to defeat China's He Jiting and Ren Xiangyu 11-21, 21-13, 21-17 in the Super 750 final. The victory is their second BWF World Tour title of 2026 and their 10th career title overall, providing a major confidence boost ahead of the Asian Games in Japan.
