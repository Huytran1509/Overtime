The 2026 BWF World Championships field features five former women's singles champions among the 16 seeds, including three-time winner Yamaguchi Akane of Japan, Ratchanok Intanon of Thailand, PV Sindhu, and Okuhara Nozomi. On the men's side, defending champion Shi Yuqi is joined by 2023 winner Kunlavut Vitidsarn of Thailand and 2021 champion Loh Kean Yew of Singapore. The competition runs through August 23, with global streaming available via BWF TV on YouTube and Olympics.com.
