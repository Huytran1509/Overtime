The Korea e-Sports Association (KeSPA) announced on November 20 that the national main event of the 2026 National Middle and High School Esports Championship will take place online from November 21 to 22. The top 32 teams across League of Legends, VALORANT, and PUBG will compete for a combined prize pool of 30 million won. All matches will be broadcast live on the official School Esports YouTube channel, SOOP, and Chzzk.
