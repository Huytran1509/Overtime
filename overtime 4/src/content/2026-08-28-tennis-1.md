Jannik Sinner has confirmed he will not compete at the 2026 US Open due to a right knee injury sustained after his Wimbledon title defence, marking the first time since 2019 that the Italian will miss a Grand Slam. Sinner, who holds a 44-3 record this year and won five Grand Slam titles, had already skipped the Masters events in Montreal and Cincinnati with the same complaint. His expected return is targeting the Asian swing, with the Beijing ATP 500 event in late September identified as a realistic comeback tournament.
