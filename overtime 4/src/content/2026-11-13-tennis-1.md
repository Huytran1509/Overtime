Jessica Pegula saved a match point to defeat Wimbledon champion Linda Noskova in a nearly three-hour thriller at the WTA Finals in Indian Wells, booking her place in the semi-finals. Pegula fired a career-high 16 aces in the 7-6, 4-6, 7-5 victory, while Noskova committed a career-high 15 double faults. The result eliminated world No. 4 Coco Gauff from the season-ending tournament despite Gauff winning her own match against Marta Kostyuk on the same day.
