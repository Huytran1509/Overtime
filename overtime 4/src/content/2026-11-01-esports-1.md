The Swiss Stage of the 2026 League of Legends World Championship concluded in Allen, Texas, with eight teams confirming their places in the quarterfinals. The bracket draw produced the following matchups: Dplus KIA versus BLG, Gen.G versus LYON, Karmine Corp versus T1, and an all-LPL tie between Top Esports and Invictus Gaming. Gen.G and DK, who both finished the Swiss Stage at 3-0, were placed on opposite sides of the bracket to prevent them meeting before the final, with quarterfinals scheduled to begin November 4.
