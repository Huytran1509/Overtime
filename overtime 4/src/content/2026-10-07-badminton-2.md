The 2026 BWF World Junior Mixed Team Championships, also known as the Suhandinata Cup, reached the quarter-final stage on October 7 at the Cairo Stadium Indoor Halls Complex in Egypt. India, who won a historic bronze medal last year, were in contention to qualify for the knock-out rounds after facing Algeria and Peru in group play. The tournament, which runs until October 10, is being played under a new 3x55 relay scoring format for the first time.
