Taiwan's Chou Tien-chen, aged 36, made history by winning the 2026 China Open men's singles title, becoming the oldest player ever to claim a BWF Super 1000 tournament crown. He defeated France's Toma Junior Popov in the final, having earlier beaten compatriot Lin Chun-yi in the semi-finals with a 21-18, 21-17 victory. The achievement is particularly notable as Chou also snapped a nine-match losing streak against world number one Shi Yuqi earlier in the tournament.
