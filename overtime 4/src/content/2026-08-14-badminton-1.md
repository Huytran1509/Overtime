The 30th edition of the BWF World Championships is set to begin on 17 August at the Indira Gandhi Arena in New Delhi, India. It marks India's first hosting of the event since 2009 and returns the tournament to Asia for the first time since 2018. All five disciplines — men's and women's singles, men's and women's doubles, and mixed doubles — will be contested across the week-long tournament.
