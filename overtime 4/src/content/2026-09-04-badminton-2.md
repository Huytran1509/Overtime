Teenager Unnati Hooda, ranked 25th in the world, was eliminated from the BWF Super 750 China Masters 2026 in Shenzhen after losing to South Korea's world No. 12 Kim Ga-eun in women's singles. The 18-year-old lost 21-10, 21-18 in 37 minutes. India's men's doubles pair of MR Arjun and Hariharan Amsakarunan also retired mid-match against a Chinese pair. Lakshya Sen and the Satwik-Chirag men's doubles pairing are among those still in contention at the event.
