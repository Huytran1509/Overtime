The 30th edition of the BWF World Championships, held at Indira Gandhi Arena in New Delhi from August 17-23, wrapped up with finals across all five disciplines. India hosted the event for the first time since 2009, with top seeds An Seyoung of South Korea and Shi Yuqi of China headlining the women's and men's singles draws respectively. The tournament drew global attention as badminton's biggest annual prize was contested in front of home fans after the sport returned to Asian soil for the first time since Nanjing 2018.
