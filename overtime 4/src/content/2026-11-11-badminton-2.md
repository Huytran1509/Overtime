South Korean world number one An Se-young has drawn comparisons to Indonesian legend Susi Susanti after achieving 11 BWF World Tour titles in a single season for the second consecutive year. An recently defeated world number two Yamaguchi Akane of Japan 2-1 in the women's singles final of a BWF event held in South Korea. The feat matches the all-time single-season record of 11 titles set by Japan's Kento Momota in 2019, which An has now equalled in back-to-back seasons. With An absent, Yamaguchi is the top seed at the ongoing Kumamoto Masters in Japan.
