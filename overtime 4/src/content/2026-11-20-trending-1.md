World Rugby has suspended Oceania Rugby from its Council membership following a governance investigation, though the regional body retains its full World Rugby membership and is cooperating with the process. The decision was announced after a World Rugby Council meeting in Dublin, which also approved Mali as a full World Rugby member and welcomed Asia Rugby back onto the Council. The suspension adds to recent governance turbulence in the Pacific region, with Samoa's Lakapi also self-suspended from the Council.
