Chinese League of Legends team Top Esports confirmed that starting top laner Cheng 'ZUIAN' Zi-Hao will be unable to travel to the United States for Worlds 2026 after failing two US visa interviews. The organization announced that veteran top laner Bai '369' Jia-Hao, who has not played a competitive match since March, will step back into the starting lineup. TES is still pursuing a third visa interview for ZUIAN, with the tournament's main stage not beginning until October 23, and reports suggest two of the team's coaches are also facing visa difficulties.
