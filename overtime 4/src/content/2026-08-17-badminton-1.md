The 30th edition of the BWF World Championships got underway on August 17 at the Indira Gandhi Arena in New Delhi, marking India's first time hosting the event since 2009. The week-long tournament features 64 players in each of the singles draws and 48 pairs across the three doubles disciplines, with opening rounds staggered across August 17 and 18. South Korea's An Se-young and China's Shi Yuqi are the top seeds in women's and men's singles respectively, with the finals scheduled for August 23.
