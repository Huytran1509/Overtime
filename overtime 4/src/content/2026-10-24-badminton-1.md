PV Sindhu kept India's campaign alive at the 2026 BWF French Open in Paris by defeating Chinese Taipei's Chiu Pin-chian 21-13, 21-14 in the women's singles quarter-finals. This was Sindhu's second consecutive win over Chiu and her fourth semi-final appearance on the BWF World Tour this year. Fellow Indian Ashmita Chaliha, who had upset world champion Akane Yamaguchi in the previous round, was eliminated at the quarter-final stage by Nozomi Okuhara.
