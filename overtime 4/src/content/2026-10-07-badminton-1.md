India's PV Sindhu advanced comfortably to the second round of the BWF Arctic Open 2026 in Vantaa, Finland, dispatching Canada's Wen Yu Zhang 21-8, 21-11 in just 28 minutes. Seeded sixth at the tournament, the two-time Olympic medallist will next face Japan's Riko Gunji. However, India's women's challenge thinned considerably as Unnati Hooda was eliminated in a gruelling three-game contest, and Ashmita Chaliha fell to seventh seed Line Christophersen in an even closer finish.
