India's Satwiksairaj Rankireddy and Chirag Shetty claimed their first-ever China Masters title, becoming the first Indian badminton pair to win the tournament since it launched in 2005. The duo came from behind to defeat Chinese pair He Jiting and Ren Xiangyu 11-21, 21-13, 21-17 in the final of the BWF Super 750 event in Shenzhen. The victory was their 10th career BWF World Tour title and their second of the 2026 season. The pair now shift focus to the Asian Games in Aichi-Nagoya, Japan, beginning September 19.
