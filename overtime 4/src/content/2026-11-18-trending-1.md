MS Dhoni officially ended his 19-year IPL playing career on November 17, with Chennai Super Kings confirming he will become the franchise's team mentor from IPL 2027. The 45-year-old, who captained CSK to five IPL titles, retired with 5,439 runs and is widely regarded as the greatest finisher in the tournament's history. Dhoni announced the decision via an Instagram story, mirroring the style of his earlier international retirement post.
