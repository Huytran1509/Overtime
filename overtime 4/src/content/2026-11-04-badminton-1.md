Two-time Olympic medallist PV Sindhu publicly urged institutional support for India's young badminton players, highlighting the financial difficulties of competing on the international circuit. She pointed to the prize money gap with tennis and stressed that shuttlers without sponsors need government assistance to cover travel and support-team costs. The appeal came after it emerged that Sindhu and Lakshya Sen had to self-fund participation in four European tournaments, as the Sports Authority of India declined to finance the trip.
