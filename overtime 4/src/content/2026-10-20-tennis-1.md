Poland's Maja Chwalinska won her maiden WTA title at the 2026 Wuhan Open, defeating Britain's Sonay Kartal 2-6, 6-4, 6-2 in a dramatic comeback final. The 25-year-old, who was ranked outside the top 100 before this season, overcame Iga Swiatek and Iva Jovic on her way to the title. The victory propels Chwalinska into the world top 11 and makes her only the fourth player ever to win a WTA 1000 event as their first tour-level title.
