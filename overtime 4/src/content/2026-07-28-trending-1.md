LeBron James, 41, has officially signed a two-year, $8 million contract with the Philadelphia 76ers for his 24th NBA season, ending months of speculation that had included the possibility of retirement. The deal is the smallest salary of James' career and represents a massive pay cut from the nearly $53 million he earned with the Los Angeles Lakers last season. The 76ers now boast two former Finals MVPs after also acquiring Jaylen Brown earlier in the offseason, and James will team up with Joel Embiid in Philadelphia's frontcourt. James described the move as his 'last decision' in professional basketball.
