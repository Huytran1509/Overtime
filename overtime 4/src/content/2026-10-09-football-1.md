UEFA president Aleksander Ceferin announced in Berlin on October 8 that he intends to stand for a fourth term as head of European football's governing body, reversing his previous public pledge in 2024 not to seek re-election. The Slovenian informed leaders of all 55 national associations of his decision, with the vote scheduled for next year's UEFA Congress in Astana, Kazakhstan. If elected, Ceferin's term would run through to 2031. His candidacy comes amid heightened tensions between UEFA and FIFA president Gianni Infantino over governance and commercial strategy.
