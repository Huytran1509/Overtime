Two-time Cy Young Award winner Tarik Skubal has agreed to a nine-year, $450 million contract with the New York Mets, the richest deal ever handed to a pitcher in Major League Baseball history. The deal surpasses Yoshinobu Yamamoto's previous pitching record of $325 million over 12 years with the Dodgers, and carries an average annual value of $50 million, also the highest ever for a pitcher. Skubal, who turned 30 last Friday, spent the final stretch of the 2026 season with the Los Angeles Dodgers after a midseason trade from Detroit, before choosing New York in free agency.
