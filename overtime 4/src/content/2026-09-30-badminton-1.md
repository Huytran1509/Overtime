Indonesia claimed the men's team badminton gold medal at the 2026 Asian Games in Aichi, Japan, defeating defending champions China 3-2 in a historic final. It was Indonesia's first men's team title since 1998, ending China's run of two consecutive gold medals. India and Thailand shared the bronze medals after being eliminated in the semi-finals.
