India has accumulated 35 medals, including nine gold, at the 2026 Asian Para Games in Aichi-Nagoya, Japan, sitting sixth in the overall standings. Standout performances on Day 2 included gold for Deepthi Jeevanji in the women's 400m T20 event and a world record in para archery by Sheetal Devi. The country is aiming to surpass its record haul of 111 medals from the 2023 Games in Hangzhou, with multiple events still to come across disciplines including para-badminton, para-swimming, and para-athletics.
