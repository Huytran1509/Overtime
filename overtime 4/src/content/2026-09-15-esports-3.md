French club AG.AL claimed the 2026 Esports World Cup Club Championship title in Paris, becoming the first new club to win the championship in EWC history. The tournament, moved from its traditional Riyadh base to Paris due to regional security concerns, featured 25 events across 24 esports titles with a total prize pool of $75 million. Team Falcons and Team Vitality finished as the first and second runners-up respectively.
