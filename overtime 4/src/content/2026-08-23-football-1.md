Liverpool midfielder Curtis Jones has completed a move to Inter Milan in a deal worth approximately £30 million, ending his 16-year association with the club. Jones, who played 228 times for Liverpool and scored 22 goals, signed a five-year contract with Inter and becomes the third England international to join the Italian side this summer, following John Stones and Djed Spence. Liverpool will receive a fixed fee with add-ons, and a 10 per cent sell-on clause has been included in the agreement.
