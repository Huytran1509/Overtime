Diego Simeone became the most successful manager in La Liga history after Atletico Madrid beat Getafe 3-0, giving him a record 345 victories in the Spanish top flight. He surpassed former Atletico legend Luis Aragones, who held the previous record of 344 wins, and notably achieved the feat in 567 matches compared to Aragones' 756. All 345 of Simeone's victories have come at a single club, Atletico Madrid, where he has also won eight major trophies since taking charge in December 2011.
