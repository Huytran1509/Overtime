World No. 1 Jannik Sinner, fresh from successfully defending his Wimbledon title, and 24-time Grand Slam champion Novak Djokovic have both withdrawn from the National Bank Open in Montreal, which begins August 1. Carlos Alcaraz had already pulled out with a wrist injury, leaving the tournament without its three biggest stars. Tournament director Valerie Tetreault called the situation an issue for the sport and said Tennis Canada is in talks with the ATP about calendar reform.
