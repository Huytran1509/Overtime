Jannik Sinner, the world's top-ranked men's tennis player, announced his withdrawal from the 2026 US Open due to a persistent right knee injury sustained since winning Wimbledon in July. The Italian had already missed the Canadian Open and Cincinnati Open as he sought treatment in Milan, and trials back on court in Monte Carlo confirmed he needs more recovery time. This is the first Grand Slam Sinner has missed since his debut at the 2019 US Open, and he is only the second ATP world No. 1 ever to skip the tournament since rankings began in 1973.
