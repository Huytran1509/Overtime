India's Lakshya Sen secured his place in the BWF World Tour Finals after compatriot Kidambi Srikanth defeated Japan's Koki Watanabe in the men's singles first round at the Hong Kong Open Super 500 on Wednesday. Sen heads into the tournament in outstanding form, having won back-to-back titles at the Hylo Open and Kumamoto Masters Japan to extend his winning streak to ten matches. He faces Denmark's Rasmus Gemke in the round of 16 on November 19 at Kai Tak Arena in Hong Kong.
